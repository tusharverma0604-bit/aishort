# ai-short-video-generator
AI Short Video Generator is an AI-powered tool that turns long YouTube videos into engaging short-form content for YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and Instagram Reels. It downloads videos, transcribes speech, identifies the most viral moments, automatically reframes clips into vertical 9:16 format, adds stylish captions, generates thumbnails, and lets creators download or upload finished shorts directly to YouTube. It helps creators save time and turn long videos into shareable content in just a few steps.
